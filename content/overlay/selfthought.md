# Self-thought

Triggered with `%selfthought <message>` for a cost of **5000 points** and **-200 karma**.

When triggered, a robo-vanor appears in the middle of the screen and speaks your message in a voice similar to vanor's.

It uses a local speech model with voice cloning to generate the audio.
The message is read in vanor's cloned voice, appearing as an overlay popup with a dismiss timer.
